# Turn your reviews into a one-page website (Claude prompt)

From the @umer.does.ai reel "A US agency charges $6,500+. Claude built this in 51 seconds."

## What you need
- Your business name, city and opening hours
- 5 to 10 real customer reviews, copied word for word (Google, Yelp, Facebook)
- Claude (claude.ai works; Claude Code also writes the file for you)

## Step 1: fill in this brief

```
Business: [NAME], [CITY]
Hours: [DAYS + TIMES]
Main action I want visitors to take: [e.g. book a table / pre-order a cake / call us]

Reviews:
1. "[paste review]" [stars]
2. "[paste review]" [stars]
3. "[paste review]" [stars]
4. "[paste review]" [stars]
5. "[paste review]" [stars]
```

## Step 2: paste this prompt under it

```
Build a one-page website for this business from its reviews.
Find what customers love most and turn it into the headline, the menu highlights and the section order.
Quote the best reviews word for word. Add hours, a map link and a "[MAIN ACTION]" button.
Mobile first, one HTML file, no stock photos, no lorem ipsum.
Don't invent facts: if something isn't in the brief or the reviews, leave it out or mark it [ADD].
```

## Step 3: two follow-ups that make it better
1. "Show me the 3 review phrases you built the page around, and why."
2. "Now give me 2 other headline options from different reviews."

## Before you put it online
- Swap in real photos of your place and your products
- Get a domain + hosting (or paste the file into any static host)
- Check every claim on the page is true
- Set up your Google Business Profile

An agency still does photos, SEO, hosting and support. This gets you a strong first draft for free.
