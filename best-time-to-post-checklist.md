# Best time to post: the 5-minute checklist

1. **Find your audience's hours.** Instagram app: Professional dashboard → Insights → Total followers → Most active times. Note the top 2-3 hours and the top city/country.
2. **Convert to your time zone.** Example: 7:00 PM in New York is 4:00 AM in Pakistan (PKT) during US summer time and 5:00 AM during US winter time. Write the converted slots down once.
3. **Write once, tweak per platform.** Same idea, different caption: hashtags and a CTA for Instagram, a question for Facebook, short and chatty for Threads.
4. **Schedule for their time, not yours.** Put each post in your scheduler at the converted slot. In ContentStudio: Composer → pick accounts → Customize per platform → set date and time → Schedule.
5. **Check after 2 weeks.** Compare reach on the scheduled slots against posts you made "whenever". Keep the slots that win and drop the rest.

Watch out: daylight saving shifts US times by an hour in March and November, so re-check your slots then.
