# Free Google AI that actually works in India (checked Oct 21, 2026)

1. **Google AI Plus, free for 12 months (college students)**: gemini.google.com/students
   Normally ₹399/month (₹4,788 a year). Verify through Google One / SheerID. Redeem by **Dec 31, 2026**. It renews at ₹399 unless you cancel.
2. **NotebookLM**: notebooklm.google.com
   Upload notes or PDFs and get an Audio Overview (a podcast). Set Output language to Hindi in settings.
3. **Stitch (Google Labs)**: stitch.withgoogle.com
   Turns one prompt into app screens and front-end code. Free while in beta.
4. **Google AI Studio**: aistudio.google.com
   Describe an app and it builds it. Free tier plus a free Gemini API key for personal projects.
5. **Jules**: jules.google
   Coding agent for your GitHub repos. Free plan: 15 tasks a day.
6. **Nano Banana (in the Gemini app)**: gemini.google.com
   Free daily image edits. Try "Add Diwali lights to my balcony".

## Traps from the viral "15 free tools" lists
- **Opal**: Google set **Nov 17, 2026** as its shutdown date and says to use Skills in Gemini instead.
- **Gemini CLI**: the free Google-login tier moved to Antigravity CLI on Jun 18, 2026.
- **Mixboard**: US-only in beta (waitlist).

Limits change often. Check each page before you rely on it.
