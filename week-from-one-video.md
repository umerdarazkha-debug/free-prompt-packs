# One video, one week of posts: the prompt pack

From @umer.does.ai. Use with Claude plus the free **youtube-decoder** skill ([get it here](https://github.com/umerdarazkha-debug/youtube-decoder)).
Rule for every step: **borrow the shape, never the words.** You are learning why a video works, not copying it.

---

## Step 1: Decode a video that's working

Paste a YouTube link (any video with captions) and say:

```
/youtube-decoder <link>

Give me the three moves that keep people watching, each with the timestamp where it happens:
1. how fast the promise lands,
2. what keeps people from leaving in the middle,
3. where the payoff is saved.
```

Example from my reel: MrBeast, "Eat Everything In A Grocery Store, Win $1,000,000" (57:59). The promise lands by 0:06, a new day milestone arrives every few minutes (day 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 100), and the payoff is saved for the final minute (57:11).

## Step 2: Rebuild the shape for your niche

```
My niche: <your niche>. My audience: <who they are>.
Keep those three moves, but write a brand-new idea for my niche. Don't reuse any of the original words, people or events.
Give me: the promise in under 12 words, 5 milestones, and the payoff.
```

## Step 3: Turn it into a week

```
Using that rebuilt structure, write my week of posts:

Mon: a 7-slide carousel. Slide 1 is the promise. Slides 2-6 are the milestones, one per slide, under 20 words each. Slide 7 is the payoff plus "save this".
     Design notes: editorial look, one strong photo per slide, lots of white space, one serif headline.
Tue: a 30-second reel script. The promise in the first line, said over one strong hero shot. Then three beats, then the payoff.
Wed, Thu, Fri: three Instagram captions on the same idea, each with a different first line (a question, a bold claim, a number). First line under 10 words. No hashtags in the body. End with one question.
Sat: a LinkedIn post. Open with the lesson, tell it as a short story, end with one takeaway. No emojis.
Sun: a short story-style recap that points back to the carousel.
```

## Step 4: Make it sound like you

```
Here are 5 of my best posts: <paste>. Rewrite every piece above so it matches their tone, sentence length and hooks.
```

## Step 5: Schedule it

Paste the finished pieces into your scheduler. I use ContentStudio: one post per day, Monday to Sunday, then approve the week in one sitting.
