# Audit your Instagram profile with AI (before you spend on ads)

From @umer.does.ai. Works with Claude or ChatGPT. Takes about 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Take 2 screenshots

1. Your profile, top of the page: photo, name, bio, link, highlights.
2. Your grid: scroll so your last 9 posts are visible.

## Step 2: Paste both into Claude with this prompt

```
You are a brutally honest Instagram strategist. I'm about to run ads that send people to this profile.
Here are 2 screenshots: my profile header and my last 9 posts.
My business: <what you sell, to whom>.

Score my profile out of 10 on each of these 5 checks, with one specific fix for each:
1. Bio: does it say what I do and who it's for in about 10 words?
2. Highlights: do the covers and names answer the questions a new visitor has (price, results, how it works, contact)?
3. Last 9 posts: do they show proof (results, testimonials, before/after) or just random content?
4. Call to action: is there one clear next step (link, button, "DM me X") that someone can actually tap?
5. Consistency: same colors, fonts and style, so the grid looks like one brand?

Then rewrite my bio 3 ways, each under 150 characters, and tell me which 3 posts to pin.
```

## Step 3: Fix the lowest score first

Don't fix everything at once. Fix the lowest score, then run the audit again next week.

## Learn from the big accounts

- **One clear ask:** MrBeast's bio is basically one instruction, watch my latest video, with the link right under it.
- **One look:** scroll Cristiano Ronaldo's or Kendall Jenner's grid: every post feels like the same brand.
- **Real moments as proof:** IShowSpeed's grid is full of real events and people, which is proof he's where the action is.
