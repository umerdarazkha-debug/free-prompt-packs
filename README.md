# free-prompt-packs

Free prompts and checklists for doing real marketing work with AI. Each one comes from a reel I made.

Open a file, copy the prompt, fill in the parts in brackets and paste it into the tool the pack names.

## Before you use them

- **Check the facts.** AI gets numbers and names wrong. Every pack tells the AI not to invent things, and you should still check the result against your source.
- **Borrow the shape, never the words.** Some packs study other people's content. Use them to learn why something works, not to copy it.
- **Dated packs go out of date.** Free offers, limits and shortcuts change. Where a pack says when it was checked, check again before you rely on it.
